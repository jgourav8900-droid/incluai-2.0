// questions & answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about INCLUAI intelligence, security, and enterprise onboarding.

What information is omitted for anonymous barrier reports? +
Anonymous submissions are stored without the submitting account ID. The report description and other submitted content are not automatically scrubbed and may identify the author.
How should I use AI Copilot answers? +
AI Copilot can search configured policy documents and may provide citations, but it does not guarantee accuracy or detect every unsupported claim. Check important information against the cited source and consult HR when needed.
Can INCLUAI integrate with Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Workday? +
Yes! INCLUAI provides REST APIs and webhook dispatchers that connect directly to enterprise communication hubs and HRIS platforms. Employees can interact with the Copilot or track accommodations without leaving their daily workflow.
Where is organization data stored? +
This prototype stores application records in local JSON files. If an external AI provider is configured, review that provider's data-handling terms before sending organization content.
How is the 2.4-day accommodation SLA tracked and enforced? +
When an accommodation is requested, the system automatically creates a tracked task assigned to facilities and HR with automated SLA alerts at 24h, 48h, and 72h. Our automated telemetry dashboard alerts leadership if any barrier request risks breach.
How long does onboarding and policy indexing take? +
Most organizations are fully operational within 24 to 48 hours. Our ingestion pipeline parses and indexes PDF manuals, Notion spaces, and Google Docs automatically with immediate vector verification.
